Joshua Kiriakoff is a SOC Analyst at Huntress in Perth, Western Australia. With a background in offensive security, he investigates intrusions, hunts threats and analyses malware, turning what he learns into better detections and defences. His work ranges from piecing together an attacker's activity to making containment decisions during active compromises. He also shares his findings through published research.
