Kerberos ticket cache purge via klist

ATT&CK · T1070.006 (Timestomp / Indicator Removal) Tactic · Credential Access Tactic · Defense Evasion
Hypothesis. klist purge flushes the current logon session's cached Kerberos tickets. Operators purge the cache to drop a stolen or forged ticket cleanly, to force fresh ticket requests after manipulating privileges, or to remove evidence of pass-the-ticket activity. Interactive purges are uncommon in normal operation.

context

The purge sits next to ticket abuse. On its own it is a maintenance command, but in an intrusion it is usually the step that clears the deck, either dropping a ticket that was injected or wiping the traces of one that was used. That is why it reads better in context than in isolation.

It is a signed, built-in tool, so nothing about the binary is suspicious. The verb and the company it keeps are the signal.

hunt

Match klist run with the purge verb.

// hypothesis: klist purge of the Kerberos ticket cache.

FROM logs-edr-process_events
| WHERE TO_LOWER(process.name) == "klist.exe"
| WHERE TO_LOWER(process.command_line) LIKE "*purge*"
| KEEP host.name, user.name, process.parent.name, process.command_line
| SORT @timestamp DESC
| LIMIT 100

findings & discriminators

  • Context makes the call. A purge beside privilege changes or ticket manipulation in the same session is the pass-the-ticket cleanup, not troubleshooting.
  • Watch the session. Correlate with adjacent logon and ticket-request activity to tell an operator apart from a help-desk fix.
