Each entry in this section is one artefact, written end-to-end as a single document: a falsifiable hypothesis, the ES|QL hunt I ran against it, the findings and discriminators that fell out, and the resulting Sigma rule.

The structure each writeup follows:

  1. Hypothesis — one sentence, falsifiable.
  2. Context — TTPs, MITRE ATT&CK technique IDs, why this matters.
  3. Hunt — runnable ES|QL against the canonical Huntress Process Insights schema.
  4. Findings & discriminators — fields that separate signal from noise.
  5. Sigma rule — the detector the hunt produced.
  6. False positives — known FPs, scoping notes.
  7. References.
