Three kinds of posts live here, split into subsections:

  • internal — essays I've written for this site.
  • external — articles I've published elsewhere under my own byline. Hover the filename to see the off-site URL (if you want).
  • contributions — write-ups published by other people that came out of incidents I worked. Sometimes that's the research behind the piece, sometimes it's the triage or the investigation that prompted it in the first place. I didn't publish these and didn't necessarily write any of them.
