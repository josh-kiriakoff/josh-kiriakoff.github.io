Most of the hunt hypotheses I test start as someone else's write-up, a new entry in the KEV catalogue or an advisory describing tradecraft I have not yet seen in the wild. This page keeps the ten most recent items from five feeds I read anyway and links straight to the source.

It refreshes once a day and takes turns across the feeds, newest item first, so a busy feed cannot drown out a quiet one. The sources are CISA cybersecurity advisories, the CISA Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalogue, the SANS Internet Storm Center, Cisco Talos and Palo Alto Networks Unit 42. The same ten entries are published as feed.json.
